AC audit & condition report

The facility assessment: every unit measured, rated and put on paper

A paid audit of your building’s air conditioning: we inventory and instrument-test every unit, then hand you a written condition report your management can act on — with us or without us. The fee is credited against an AMC signed within 30 days.

The process

Four steps, one document.

  1. Inventory

    Every unit tagged and logged: location, type, tonnage, brand, approximate age, serial where legible.

  2. Measure

    Refrigerant pressure against manufacturer spec, capacitor microfarads against rating, current draw, coil condition, drain flow, electrical state — per unit, with instruments.

  3. Grade and prioritise

    Each unit gets a condition grade and each finding a priority: fix now, plan this quarter, budget this year. Remaining useful life estimated for ageing units.

  4. Report and debrief

    The written report is handed over and walked through with your team — what it says, what it costs to act on, and in what order.

Who books this

Buildings that are done guessing.

Facility managers taking over a site with no maintenance records. Admin heads who suspect their “serviced” units haven’t been opened in years. Buyers doing diligence on a property with a large installed AC estate. Managements comparing an incumbent contractor’s claims against measured reality.

The report ends the argument either way: it is the baseline every later decision — repair, replace, contract — gets measured against.

FAQ

Assessment — specifics.

What exactly do we receive?

A written, per-unit condition report for every air conditioner on the site: identity and location, measured refrigerant pressure against specification, capacitor reading, coil condition ratings, electrical state, an overall condition grade, and a prioritised action list with what to do now, what to plan, and what to budget. The format is here.

Why is the assessment paid?

Because it is the product, not the pitch. A free survey is a sales visit; a paid audit obliges us to measure everything and put our name on the findings. The fee is credited against the AMC if you sign within 30 days — so if the audit leads to a contract, it costs nothing.

How long does it take?

Depends on unit count and access — a 10-unit clinic is typically a half day on site; a 60-unit building takes longer. The written report follows within days, and we walk your team through it.

Do we have to sign an AMC afterwards?

No. The report is yours either way — including if you hand it to another contractor to execute. Buildings that see the readings tend to stop guessing about their AC estate, and what happens next is your call.

Is this an energy audit?

It overlaps. Fouled coils, low gas and failing components are exactly what drives AC power consumption up 15–30%. The report flags the units burning money and what fixing them involves — see how much a dirty coil actually wastes.
