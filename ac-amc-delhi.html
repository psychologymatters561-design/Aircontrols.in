Why an AC AMC is a necessity, not an extra

Think of AMC the way you think of a car service: skipping it does not save money, it defers a bigger bill to a worse time. Here is what an unmaintained AC costs you — quietly, every month:

  • Higher electricity bills. A dirty condenser coil can raise power consumption by 15–25%. Over a Delhi summer that is often more than the entire AMC fee.
  • Peak-season breakdowns. Compressors, capacitors and PCBs fail under load — in May, not December. Emergency repairs cost 2–4× a planned service, if you can even get a technician the same day.
  • Shorter AC life. Neglected units die years earlier. Maintenance is the cheapest way to protect a ₹40,000–₹1,50,000 asset.
  • Poor air quality. Clogged filters push dust, allergens and damp smells into rooms your family breathes in.

What every Air Control AMC includes

Two scheduled deep services a year — pre-summer (March–April) and post-monsoon (September–October) — plus priority response whenever something goes wrong in between. Every visit runs our full 9-point protocol:

  1. Evaporator & condenser coils deep-cleaned
  2. Air filters washed / serviced
  3. Condensate drain flushed and checked for leaks
  4. Refrigerant (gas) pressure measured against specification
  5. Electrical connections tightened and inspected
  6. Capacitor and compressor health recorded
  7. Fan motor and blades checked and cleaned
  8. Thermostat calibrated and cooling performance verified
  9. Written condition report of the unit
