Why March beats June

Most summer breakdowns are decided in winter.

The AC that dies on a 45°C afternoon usually spent months warning about it — a coil fouling, a capacitor drifting, gas slowly leaking. A pre-summer service catches all three for less than the cost of the emergency call-out, and in March you are not competing with half of Delhi for a technician.

FAQ

Home AC service — the usual questions.

What does a home AC AMC cost in Delhi?

Plans start from ₹1,800 per AC per year for a newer 1-ton split on the basic (non-comprehensive) plan, and rise with tonnage, unit age and cover level — a comprehensive plan on an ageing 1.5-ton unit costs several times that, because it covers parts and gas. The calculator gives you your exact number in under a minute, and multi-AC homes get volume discounts.

What are normal AC servicing charges in Delhi?

A basic service visit runs ₹499–₹799; a deep clean with coil wash ₹800–₹1,500; gas refilling ₹1,500–₹2,500 depending on refrigerant. The full menu is on the service charges page. Prices are confirmed before any work starts.

Is an AMC worth it for a home with 2–3 ACs?

Usually, yes — Delhi ACs run 8+ hours a day for over half the year, and a single peak-summer breakdown (compressor, PCB) costs more than years of plan fees. The plan also means your pre-summer service actually happens, in March, before the queues. The honest arithmetic is in our pricing guide — including when an AMC is not worth it.

How fast do you come when the AC dies in June?

Plan members get a 4-hour residential response commitment and skip the open queue. Non-members are served same-day in most cases. We run 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM, every day of the year, including Sundays and holidays.

Do you top up gas without checking for leaks?

No. If gas is low, it leaked, and topping up without repairing the leak means paying for gas twice. We find and fix the leak, pressure-test, then recharge to specification — R22, R410A or R32.

Which brands and AC types do you service at home?

All major brands and all common types — split, window and cassette. Every repair carries a 90-day workmanship warranty; parts carry their manufacturer warranty.
