Rates apply to standard residential split and window units across Delhi NCR. Gas is refilled only after the leak is found and repaired — a top-up without the repair wastes your money and we don’t sell it. Cassette, ducted and commercial work is quoted per site: see AC repair for businesses.

Servicing twice a year costs less than fixing once a summer.

A pre-summer deep clean plus a mid-season check costs about what a single gas-and-capacitor emergency does — and the emergency arrives with three hot days of waiting attached in June. If you own more than one AC, a maintenance plan is usually the cheaper year.

Why is there a price range instead of one price?

Because the work varies honestly: a lightly used 1-ton split deep-cleans faster than a grease-loaded 2-ton, and gas charges depend on refrigerant type and quantity after the leak is repaired. You get the exact figure before work starts — the ranges are the fence, not the fine print.

Is diagnosis charged?

The visit charge covers diagnosis with instruments — pressures, electrical readings, coil inspection. If you approve the repair, the work is quoted before it begins. No repair is started on a verbal maybe.

Do plan members pay these rates?

Plan members’ scheduled services and covered items are included in the plan; anything outside plan scope is quoted at these rates with the plan’s parts discount applied. See the plans.

When is a repair not worth doing?

When the repair cost approaches half the price of an equivalent new unit on an old, inefficient AC — typically a compressor failure past year ten. We tell you that before quoting, and the reasoning is in repair or replace.

Want the exact number?

Tell us the unit, age and problem — we’ll confirm the charge before we come.
