What a proper installation includes

  1. Site survey — indoor/outdoor placement, piping route and drainage planned before anything is drilled.
  2. Secure mounting — indoor bracket level, outdoor unit on a sound, vibration-free base.
  3. Copper piping to spec — correct gauge, insulated, flared properly, minimal bends.
  4. Electrical & drain — correct wiring, earthing and a drain that actually drains.
  5. Vacuuming & leak check — lines evacuated and pressure-tested before gas is released.
  6. Commissioning — cooling verified, and the whole job documented on handover.

Why installation quality is worth paying for

  • No slow gas leak from a bad flare that empties your AC over one season.
  • Full cooling & efficiency from correctly vacuumed, correctly sized lines.
  • Longer compressor life from a properly levelled, well-ventilated outdoor unit.
  • Warranty protected — poor third-party fitting can void your brand warranty; ours doesn't.
