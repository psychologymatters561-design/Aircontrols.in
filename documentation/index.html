Reports & records

The documentation is the product

Anyone can say the maintenance was done. Our clients can show it. This page lays out every document an Air Control contract produces — the condition report format, the scope-of-work, and the vendor pack procurement asks for.

Document 1

The per-unit condition report.

Issued for every unit at every scheduled visit. One page per AC; a consolidated summary and prioritised action list for the estate. This is the full field set:

Why buyers ask for this page

A maintenance contract is invisible when it works — the report is how you verify you are getting what you pay for. It is also how you catch what you are not getting from an incumbent: ask your current contractor for their last condition report, and compare it to this field set.

Reports accumulate into per-unit histories, so decisions like repair vs replace get made on trend data rather than opinion.

Documents 2 & 3

Scope-of-work and the vendor pack.

AMC scope-of-work

What the contract commits Air Control to, in writing: the unit inventory it covers, the visit calendar, the 9-point protocol per visit, response times and escalation path, what each tier includes and excludes, and how parts and refrigerant are handled and billed. No verbal scope — if it isn’t in the document, it isn’t in the contract.

Vendor documentation pack

For procurement and vendor registration: company profile and history since 1987, registered address, GST invoicing details, service capability (brands, system types, coverage area, working hours), SLA terms and escalation matrix, and reference documentation. Requested routinely by admin and purchase teams — ask via the contact page and we’ll send it directly.
