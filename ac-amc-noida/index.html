The territory

Noida’s commercial geography runs in distinct belts. The IT and back-office blocks of Sectors 62, 63 and 65 fill with mid-rise campuses and floor-plate offices; Sector 18 is the retail heart — the Atta market grid and the mall cluster beside it; Sector 16A’s Film City houses studios and media offices; and the expressway corridor from Sector 125 onward carries corporate campuses and universities. Between them sit the older industrial sectors, with process floors and their attached offices.

Air Control contracts and services across these belts from our base at East of Kailash, South Delhi — across the Kalindi Kunj or DND crossings, with scheduled work planned around each sector’s hours and the 2-hour commercial SLA applying to breakdown calls across our Noida coverage.

What Noida’s stock asks of AC maintenance

  • Campus-scale office estates. The Sector 62/63 belt runs mixed estates at scale — VRF and ducted plant on the open floors, splits in cabins and server rooms, all accumulated across fit-outs. That is inventory-first territory: commercial AC AMC with zone scheduling and consolidated reporting.
  • Construction dust. Noida is the NCR’s most active construction market, and its condensers pay for that daily. Coils foul faster here; cleaning cycles are set from the measured coil grades, not from a citywide default.
  • Retail peaks in Sector 18. The Atta grid and the mall cluster live on footfall that peaks in the exact weeks cooling is most stressed. Retail floors are serviced before opening hours, and the pre-summer cycle lands in February–March.
  • Institutions on the expressway. Universities, training campuses and studios run large estates with usage that swings by academic and production calendars — the visit schedule is built around those rhythms, with high-duty spaces (labs, server rooms, edit suites) on tighter cycles.

How contracts run here

Every contract starts with a baseline assessment: each unit tagged, measured and graded, so the estate’s real condition is on paper before the schedule is set. From there: minimum two scheduled services a year per unit, tighter cycles where duty or dust demands it, written per-unit reports each cycle, and breakdown dispatch under the written SLA. FAQ

Local specifics.

Which parts of Noida do you cover?

The commercial spine: Sectors 62, 63 and 65’s IT and office blocks, Sector 18 and the Atta market retail district, Sector 16 and Film City in 16A, the institutional belt along the expressway including Sectors 125–135, and the industrial sectors’ office and process spaces. Greater Noida and Indirapuram enquiries are taken case by case on scheduling.

Construction dust is constant near our sector. How much does it matter?

A lot. Noida’s active construction belt loads condenser coils faster than almost anywhere in the NCR, and a dust-blinded condenser runs hot, draws 15–30% more power and shortens compressor life. Estates near active construction go on shorter coil-cleaning cycles — the condition report’s coil grades tell us, and you, when the cycle needs tightening.

We are an institute with labs and lecture halls. Can the schedule follow the academic calendar?

Yes — education and training campuses are common contract work here, and the natural service windows (breaks, exam periods, weekends) are written into the visit calendar rather than fought against.
