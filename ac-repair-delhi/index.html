Common failures

What breaks, and what fixing it involves.

Not cooling / warm air
Usually a fouled coil, a refrigerant leak or a weak compressor. We measure pressures against spec before concluding anything. The 9 causes, in order.
Won’t switch on / trips MCB
Capacitor, PCB or wiring fault. Capacitors are measured in microfarads, not judged by appearance.
Gas leak
Leak found and repaired first, system pressure-tested, then recharged to specification — R22, R410A or R32. Never a blind top-up.
Water leaking indoors
Blocked condensate drain or a frozen evaporator coil. Drain flushed and flow verified; freezing traced to its cause.
Noise / vibration
Fan balance, bearings, mounts or a strained compressor — identified by where and when the noise occurs.
Error codes
Sensor, communication or refrigerant faults on inverter and VRV/VRF systems, read out and traced with the service manual, not guesswork.

FAQ

AC repair — the questions we get on the phone.

My AC is not cooling. What is the most likely cause?

In order of likelihood in Delhi: a fouled condenser coil, low refrigerant from a slow leak, a clogged filter, a weak capacitor, or a failing compressor. We diagnose with gauges and a multimeter before touching anything, and quote before repairing. The full breakdown is in why your AC stopped cooling.

Do you top up gas without finding the leak?

No. Refrigerant does not get consumed — if it is low, it leaked. Topping up without repairing the leak buys weeks, then you pay for gas twice. We find the leak, repair it, pressure-test, then recharge to manufacturer specification.

How fast can you come?

Same-day across Delhi NCR in most cases, 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM, seven days a week. AMC contract sites carry a written 2-hour commercial / 4-hour residential response commitment and are dispatched first in peak season.

What warranty do I get on a repair?

90 days on workmanship, on every repair. Replaced parts additionally carry the manufacturer’s warranty.

Which brands and AC types do you repair?

All major brands and every common type — window, split, cassette, ducted and VRV/VRF. We stock R22, R410A and R32 refrigerants.

Is it worth repairing an old AC, or should I replace it?

Depends on age, the failed component and the unit’s efficiency. A 12-year-old unit needing a compressor usually is not worth it; the same unit needing a capacitor usually is. We tell you which case you are in before quoting — the reasoning is in repair or replace.
