The territory

Gurugram is the newest building stock in the NCR — and the most demanding of its air conditioning. The corporate corridors of Cyber City, Golf Course Road, MG Road and Sohna Road run on sealed glass towers where cooling is not an amenity but the building’s life support: windows do not open, facades take full afternoon sun, and floors are dense with people and equipment. Around them sit Udyog Vihar’s industrial-office mix, the malls of MG Road, and thousands of smaller offices, clinics and restaurants in commercial complexes across the DLF phases and the new sectors.

Air Control contracts and services across these corridors from our Delhi base at East of Kailash — scheduled work is planned, and for breakdowns the 2-hour commercial SLA applies across our Gurugram coverage.

What Gurugram’s stock asks of AC maintenance

  • VRF-heavy modern estates. Much of the post-2005 stock runs VRV/VRF plant with cassette and ducted indoor units — efficient when healthy, expensive when neglected, and dependent on refrigerant charge being exactly right across long pipe runs. These systems reward the measured, recorded servicing our protocol is built on, and punish the annual once-over.
  • Solar gain with no headroom. A glass facade in a Gurugram June leaves no spare capacity: a unit 10–15% down from a fouled coil, which an older building would carry, shows up here as a hot zone by 2 pm. Pre-summer servicing is the difference between a facade the estate can hold and one it cannot.
  • Power cycling. DG changeovers and summer grid events subject compressors and control boards to repeated voltage stress. Capacitors drift; PCBs fail early. Our per-visit capacitor readings and electrical checks exist for exactly this failure pattern.
  • Multi-tenant, multi-stakeholder buildings. Corporate parks split responsibility between building management (central plant) and tenants (their own floors). Our contracts cover the tenant’s estate with per-unit reporting — and the report doubles as your evidence in any conversation with building management about what their plant is or is not delivering. FAQ

Local specifics.

Which parts of Gurugram do you cover?

The commercial corridors: Cyber City and DLF Phases 1–5, Golf Course Road and Golf Course Extension, MG Road, Sohna Road, Udyog Vihar and Sector 44’s institutional belt, plus surrounding office and retail pockets.

Our office is a glass-fronted floor on Golf Course Road and the west side never cools evenly. Is that a maintenance problem?

Often it is both a load problem and a maintenance problem. Glass facades take heavy afternoon solar gain, and marginal units serving those zones — slightly low on charge, coils slightly fouled — lose exactly the capacity headroom the facade demands. The assessment separates the two causes: what servicing will recover, and what is genuinely a design limit. You get both in writing.

Frequent DG changeovers — do they affect the AC estate?

Yes. Every changeover is a voltage event, and compressors and PCBs accumulate that stress. Capacitor readings and electrical checks each visit are how the damage shows up early — it is one of the quiet reasons Gurugram estates benefit from measured maintenance rather than casual servicing.
