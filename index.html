Questions buyers actually ask

AC AMC in Delhi — the practical answers.

What is AMC for AC?

AMC stands for Annual Maintenance Contract — a fixed-fee agreement under which a contractor services your air conditioners on a set schedule through the year, instead of you calling someone after a breakdown. A proper AC AMC covers scheduled servicing, priority breakdown response and written records of every unit’s condition. Air Control has been running maintenance contracts in Delhi NCR since 1987.

What is covered in an AC annual maintenance contract?

Every Air Control contract covers scheduled service visits running the full 9-point protocol (coils, filter, drain, electricals, capacitor reading, refrigerant pressure against specification, fan motor, thermostat calibration), a written condition report on every unit, and priority breakdown response. Whether parts and refrigerant gas are also covered depends on the tier — see the difference between comprehensive and non-comprehensive AMC.

What is the AC AMC price per unit in Delhi?

Commercial contracts are priced per unit after a site assessment, because a 10-year-old ducted system and a 2-year-old split carry very different maintenance loads. The price depends on unit type and tonnage, age, count and cover level. Our guide to AC AMC prices in Delhi explains what moves the number. For home plans, see Air Control for Homes.

Is AC AMC worth it?

An AMC pays for itself on the first breakdown it prevents. A fouled condenser coil makes the compressor draw 15–30% more power; a failing capacitor left unmeasured takes the compressor with it in peak June. Most summer breakdowns are decided in winter — by coils nobody cleaned and readings nobody took. A contract converts an unpredictable emergency into a scheduled, budgeted cost.

What is the difference between comprehensive and non-comprehensive AC AMC?

A non-comprehensive AMC covers scheduled servicing and labour; parts and refrigerant gas are billed separately as needed. A comprehensive AMC also covers parts and gas within agreed limits, so your annual cost is close to fully fixed. Comprehensive costs more per unit per year; non-comprehensive is cheaper but leaves parts exposure with you. The full comparison is here.

How many services in AC AMC per year?

Air Control contracts include a minimum of two scheduled services per unit per year — a pre-summer service (February–March) and a post-monsoon service — plus breakdown visits as needed. High-duty sites such as server rooms, cold storage and hospitals are put on quarterly or monthly schedules. The schedule is written into the contract.

Is gas charging included in AC AMC?

In a non-comprehensive contract, refrigerant gas is billed separately after a leak is found and repaired. In comprehensive tiers, gas top-ups are included within the agreed scope. Either way, we measure and record refrigerant pressure against manufacturer specification at every visit, so a slow leak is caught before the unit stops cooling.

Yes. Air Control maintains every common system type — window, split, cassette, ducted and VRV/VRF — across all major brands, on the same protocol and the same written reporting. Mixed estates (a building running splits, cassettes and a ducted floor together) are covered under one contract.
