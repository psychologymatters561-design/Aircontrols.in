The company

Air Control has been maintaining Delhi’s air conditioning since 1987

Thirty-eight-plus years of the same work from the same city: keeping air conditioners running in Delhi’s climate, on schedules, with the readings written down.

1987 to now

Air Control was established in New Delhi in 1987 and has operated continuously since, based in East of Kailash in South Delhi. The company has worked through every generation of air conditioning Delhi has installed: window units, the first split systems, cassettes and ducted plant, and today’s inverter and VRV/VRF systems — and through every refrigerant transition from R22 to R410A to R32.

The business built itself on maintenance contracts — buildings that need their air conditioning to work through a Delhi summer, not to be repaired after it fails. That focus has not changed in 38+ years. Contracts for offices, healthcare facilities, hospitality, cold storage and institutions remain the core of the work, alongside breakdown repair and installation across Delhi NCR.

How we work

Two habits define the company, and both are older than the website you are reading.

Measurement over reassurance. Every service visit runs a fixed 9-point protocol, and the point is the readings: refrigerant pressure against the manufacturer’s specification, capacitor microfarads against the rated value, coil condition, current draw. A serviced unit is one whose numbers are on record — not one that got wiped down.

Paper over promises. Contract clients receive written condition reports on every unit — you don’t get a phone call saying it’s done, you get the readings. The documentation page shows exactly what that looks like.

Technical scope

Air Control services all major brands — Daikin, Voltas, Blue Star, Carrier, Hitachi, LG, Samsung, Panasonic, O’General, Mitsubishi Electric, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Godrej, Whirlpool, Haier, Lloyd, Trane, York and McQuay, among others — across every common system type: split, window, cassette, ducted and VRV/VRF. The trade classifies this work as HVAC maintenance; our clients call it what it is — keeping the air conditioning running. Refrigerants R22, R410A and R32 are stocked and handled to specification, and repairs carry a 90-day workmanship warranty.
