AC AMC in Delhi: what a maintenance contract should actually give you

An Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) puts every air conditioner in your building on a fixed service schedule — measured, documented and backed by a written response SLA. Air Control has run AC maintenance contracts in Delhi NCR since 1987.

The argument in one line

Most summer breakdowns are decided in winter.

The compressor that dies in June was killed in January — by a fouled coil that made it draw 15–30% more power for months, or a capacitor that had drifted far from its rated microfarads with nobody measuring it. By the time an AC stops cooling, the cheap fix expired long ago.

An AMC exists to catch those failures while they are still small, on a schedule, with the readings on paper. An AMC pays for itself on the first breakdown it prevents — and in a Delhi summer, that breakdown was coming.

Cover levels

Comprehensive, semi-comprehensive, non-comprehensive.

Same protocol and reporting on every tier. The difference is who carries the parts risk.

Non-comprehensive

Scheduled services and all labour covered, breakdown visits included. Parts and refrigerant gas billed separately as needed, against the report’s findings. Lowest annual cost; best for newer estates.

Semi-comprehensive

Everything in non-comprehensive, plus minor parts (capacitors, relays, filters) and a gas top-up allowance included. The middle path most offices take.

Comprehensive

Parts and gas covered within agreed limits — your annual cost is close to fully fixed and the repair risk sits with us. Priced higher per unit; usually right for ageing estates and zero-tolerance sites.

Exclusions are written, not discovered later: compressor replacement and damage from external causes are quoted separately on every tier, in advance.

Pricing

What moves the AC AMC price per unit.

We quote after walking your site, because these five variables change the number honestly:

  1. System type and tonnage

    A 1.5-ton split and a 10-ton ducted package are different machines with different service loads.

  2. Age of the estate

    Units past year seven need more corrective work; the contract has to carry that honestly rather than hide it.

  3. Unit count

    Per-unit rates fall as count rises — travel and scheduling overheads spread across the estate.

  4. Cover level

    Non-comprehensive to comprehensive — the more parts risk we carry, the higher the per-unit rate.

  5. Duty cycle

    An AC running 8 hours a day in an office ages differently from one holding a server room at 22°C around the clock.

“Our building already has a guy”

Ask him for the last condition report.

Not the invoice — the report. Which units were serviced, what the refrigerant pressures read against spec, which capacitors are drifting, what he recommends doing before May. If that document exists, you have a maintenance programme and should keep it. If it doesn’t, you have a phone number that shows up after things break.

“We just call when it breaks” has a price too: an emergency compressor replacement in peak June — parts, urgency premium, and days of downtime while the office cooks — typically costs more than a year of contract cover for several units. The arithmetic is in the pricing guide.

“What if you don’t turn up?”

The SLA is written, with an escalation path.

Every commercial contract carries the response commitment in writing — 2 hours for commercial sites — a named point of contact, and an escalation path if the commitment slips. You are never in an open queue behind one-time callers, and in peak season that priority is most of what you are buying.

FAQ

AC annual maintenance contracts — specifics.

What are typical AC AMC charges in Delhi?

Per unit per year, the figure depends on five things: system type and tonnage, unit age, how many units are on the contract, the cover level (non-comprehensive, semi-comprehensive or comprehensive), and the duty cycle of the site. That is why we quote after an assessment rather than from a rate card. The guide to AC AMC prices in Delhi walks through each factor with numbers.

How many service visits does the AMC include per year?

A minimum of two scheduled services per unit — pre-summer (February–March) and post-monsoon — plus breakdown visits. Server rooms, cold storage and clinical spaces are scheduled quarterly or monthly. The visit calendar is written into the contract, and every visit runs the full 9-point protocol.

Is gas charging included in the AMC?

Depends on the tier. Non-comprehensive: gas is billed separately, after the leak is found and repaired — never topped up blind. Semi-comprehensive and comprehensive tiers include gas top-ups within the agreed scope. Refrigerant pressure is measured and recorded at every visit on every tier.

Comprehensive or non-comprehensive — which should we take?

Newer estates (under 5–7 years) usually run cheapest on non-comprehensive, because parts failures are rare. Ageing estates and sites that cannot tolerate downtime are usually better on comprehensive, because it fixes your annual cost and puts the repair risk on us. The full comparison is here.

Can one contract cover different AC types and multiple floors or buildings?

Yes. Most of our contracts are mixed estates — splits and cassettes in cabins, ducted or VRV/VRF plant on open floors, window units in utility rooms. One contract, one inventory, one report covering every unit, across floors or multiple sites.

What happens if a unit fails between scheduled visits?

You call, we dispatch — 2-hour response for commercial contract sites, written into the SLA with a named escalation path. Breakdown labour is covered under every tier; parts follow the tier terms.

Is there a minimum number of ACs for a commercial AMC?

No hard minimum — a six-unit clinic and a 200-unit campus both get contracts. Per-unit pricing improves with count, because travel and scheduling overheads spread across the estate. For a single home AC, see Air Control for Homes.

How do we start?

Book a facility assessment. We inventory and measure every unit, hand you a written baseline report, then quote the contract against it. The assessment fee is credited against the AMC if you sign within 30 days.
